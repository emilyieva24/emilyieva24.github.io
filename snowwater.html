Shifts in the California Snow-to-Rain Ratio

This project examined changes in California's snow-to-rain ratio across historical and future climate periods, with a focus on implications for water resource management.

Project Overview

The analysis combined precipitation, snowfall, and temperature datasets with hydrologic basin boundaries to evaluate spatial and temporal shifts in snow and rain across California.

Spatial Analysis

Tools & Methods

ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS StoryMaps, raster analysis, multidimensional raster processing, zonal statistics, and data visualization.
